How to format your data

Enter one value per month or weekday, separated by commas, spaces, or new lines. You can also paste rows from a spreadsheet, with optional row and column labels.

Each row becomes a month or weekday. Extra values create columns. Empty cells count as zero. Values must be zero or positive.
